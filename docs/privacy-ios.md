# Need More Tokens for iPhone: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 23, 2026_

Need More Tokens for iPhone ("the app") shows the AI usage limits that the Need More Tokens Mac app reads, and optionally your Claude usage, read on the iPhone itself.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers operated by us. We never receive your data.

## Where your data goes

- **iCloud (your private database).** If you turn on *Sync to iPhone* in the Mac app, it saves one small record, your latest usage snapshot, to your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database. The snapshot contains usage percentages, reset times, plan names, and the name of your Mac. The snapshot itself is stored as an end-to-end encrypted field. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
- **Provider services.** If you sign in to Claude on the iPhone, the app connects directly to Anthropic to read your usage, using an access token kept in the iPhone's Keychain on that device only. The app never sends it anywhere else. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that connection.
- **On your device.** The app keeps its latest reading in its App Group container so its widgets can show it.

## Your choices

Turn off *Sync to iPhone* on the Mac, sign out of Claude in the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove its local data. You can delete the iCloud record in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.
